# Review: colour on components, 2026-11-08

First design pass on the house theme. Every page in both themes at 390x844
and 1440x900, plus the linked-to states, measured in a headless browser on
the built site. Contrast and lightness (CIE L*) computed from token values.

## Seen, ranked

1. **Link and annotation share a lightness in dark.** Dark `--accent`
   (#8ab4ff) is L* 73, dark `--mark` (#b4a6ff) L* 72, so they differ by hue
   alone. In light they sit 12 L* apart. Token change: proposed upstream.
2. **One number wore three faces.** Inline chips were filled violet, key
   numbers a violet outline, photo marks a paper disc. Fill meant "linked to"
   on the key and photo but "at rest" in prose.
3. **Portrait figures outgrew a desk screen.** Fig. 1's plate was 896x1195 on
   a 900-high viewport; marks, caption and key never shared a view.
4. **Header rule overhung the text** by the page padding on each side; the
   footer hairline too.
5. **Sidenote 2 left a ~330 px gap.** Its thumbnail stretched one grid row.
6. **Figure references came in three forms:** violet caps in prose, a plain
   accent link in fig. 3's caption, plain text in a key gloss.
7. **Draft note and editorial slip were tinted panels,** which rule 2 rules
   out; the draft note also stopped short of the text column.
8. **`<mark>` wore a violet tint** while pointing at nothing.
9. **The vocabulary section's copy** says violet marks the linked-to state;
   on the page it was the resting face. Fix 2 makes it closer to true; the
   prose is the author's to settle.

Retracted on a closer look: photo marks on phones are 34 px, but their
`::before` pads the hit area to 48 px, above `--target`.

## Changed

- 2: chips are a violet outline at rest and fill on hover and focus; key
  numbers already filled on `:target`. One rule: outline at rest, fill when
  pointed at.
- 3: figure images cap at `100svh` less two `--room-h2-above`; the plate
  shrinks with the image, so mark percentages hold. Fig. 1 is now 615x820 on
  desk; fig. 3's zoomed crop opts out.
- 4: header and footer rules drawn as a background inside the padding.
- 5: a sidenote with a thumbnail spans three grid rows. Gap ~330 to ~220 px;
  the rest is fig. 3, which spans both columns.
- 6: caption links to `#f…` targets take the `.figref` style.
- 7: draft note is a caps DRAFT label in `--warn` between `--warn` hairlines,
  full column width; `aside.ed` takes the same shape in grey.
- 8: `<mark>` uses `--grey-tint`.

## Left, and why

- 1, overlay tokens, Inter Italic: theme changes, proposed upstream.
- 9: author's prose.
- The key gloss "detail in fig. 3" stays plain text: making it a link is a
  content edit.
- Home and About leave the right of a desk empty: the measure doing its job.
- Phone sidenotes keep a violet left rule; worth a look next round.
